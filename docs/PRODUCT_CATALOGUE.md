## 2. Specification Discrepancies & Cross-Document Clarifications

> [!WARNING]
> **Explicit Source Divergences Flagged for Confirmation:**
>
> 1. **Single Facer Model Variations (`SF-280` vs `SF-10` vs `SF-360S`):**
>    - `SF280 Single facer...pdf` specifies: Corrugator roll $\phi 280 \text{ mm}$, 48CrMo steel, ABB 10 KW motor, 15 KW total power, economic speed 80–100 m/min.
>    - `WJ-100-1800 2PLY...pdf` specifies: `SF-10` with corrugator roll $\phi 292 \text{ mm}$, 50CrMo steel, tungsten carbide coating, Hengshui 7.5 KW motor, 17 KW total power, economic speed 60–80 m/min.
>    - Legacy website mentions `SF-280S / 320S / 360S`.
>    - **Status:** These are distinct models with different roll sizes and metallurgy. Both will be maintained as distinct catalog entries.
>
> 2. **Hydraulic Mill Roll Stand Specifications (`ZJ-V5B` vs Standalone Document):**
>    - In `Hydraulic Mill Roll Stand.pdf`: Max working widths are **1600 mm** and **1400 mm**; main shaft diameter is **$\phi 219 \text{ mm}$**; supporting weight is **2 Tons per side**; electricals by **Schneider**.
>    - In `WJ-100-1800 2PLY...pdf` (`ZJ-V5B`): Max working width is **1800 mm**; main shaft diameter is **$\phi 242 \text{ mm}$**; weight capacity is **3 Tons per side**; electricals by **SIEMENS**; hydraulic station by **7 Ocean Taiwan**.
>    - **Status:** Confirmed as two different duty-rating models: `Standard/Medium Duty (2 Ton / $\phi 219$)` and `Heavy Duty ZJ-V5B (3 Ton / $\phi 242$)`.
